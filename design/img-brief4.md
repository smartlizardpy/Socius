These are hero illustrations for two new onboarding screens in a sports app called Avenza, and

## Palette — these hex values only, nothing else

ink `#101A2B`, deep blue `#0B2E7A`, blue `#1350C8`, sky `#7FA6F0`, blue tint `#E3ECFF`,
orange `#F0651C`, deep orange `#B8430C`, apricot `#FFB27A`, orange tint `#FFE7D6`,
yellow `#F7C23B`, deep yellow `#D89A12`, white `#FFFFFF`.

Blue and orange lead. Yellow is the third voice — genuinely present, roughly a fifth of the
colour weight, never a garnish and never the dominant hue.

## Hard style contract

- Flat geometric vector look. Bold confident shapes, generous scale, sports-pictogram energy
  meeting modern editorial illustration. Joyful and kinetic.
- NO gradients, NO drop shadows, NO blurs, NO textures, NO outline around the whole scene.
- NO text, letters, numerals, emoji, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.
- NO photorealism, no 3D rendering, no isometric perspective, no drop-shadowed "3D icon" style.
- Fully transparent background. No frame, no card, no ground-plane rectangle.
- People are simple and stylised — geometric bodies, no facial detail beyond the simplest
  suggestion. Mixed body types and skin tones drawn in flat palette colours.
- Roughly 1400x1050, landscape, subject centred with generous breathing room at the edges.

## The two images

1. `design/assets/gen/onb-place.png` — WHERE YOU PLAY.
   A stylised top-down neighbourhood: a few blocks of a coastal city district drawn as flat
   geometric shapes. Streets in white and pale blue tint, small buildings and trees as simple
   blocks, a strip of sea along one edge in blue. Scattered across it, three or four bold
   orange location pins mark courts and pitches — one pin larger and centred, the others
   smaller. Around the largest pin, two concentric circles suggest a radius. Small stylised
   figures walking or cycling between them. The feeling is "everything is close" — warm and
   walkable, not a technical map. No street names, no compass, no text of any kind.

2. `design/assets/gen/onb-account.png` — KEEPING YOUR PLACE.
   Two or three stylised players mid-motion — one serving, one running — with their gear, and
   beside them a large simple shield or badge shape in blue holding a bold orange check mark.
   Around it, a few small floating cards suggesting saved things: a level indicator drawn as
   simple stars, a small calendar block, a heart. The feeling is "what you set up is kept
   safe" — confident and warm rather than corporate or security-themed. No padlocks, no keys,
   no fingerprints, no shields with keyholes.
