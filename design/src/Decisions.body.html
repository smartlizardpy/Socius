AND THREE MORE
Bricolage Grotesque, not Inter
Inter is the typeface every AI-built app arrives in. Bricolage has real character in a headline and stays plain and quiet at body size — so the app has a voice without shouting.
Photographs for games, drawings for ideas
A padel court is a photograph — real light, real people. “Matched by level” is not a photograph, so it is drawn. Mixing the two on purpose is what stops it looking like a template.
Candid avatars, not studio headshots
The first set were studio portraits on a grey backdrop and everyone clocked them as fake immediately. Real people upload a photo a friend took at the court.
