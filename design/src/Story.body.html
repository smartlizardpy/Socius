Barcelona to GS, Liverpool to Fenerbahçe

[[icon:sparkle:15]] Summarised by Socius · 28 August

The Champions League league phase draw was made on Thursday evening and both Turkish clubs learned all eight opponents. Galatasaray host Barcelona at home and travel to Paris Saint-Germain.

Fenerbahçe, through from the play-off round, drew Liverpool and Roma at home and Atletico Madrid and Aston Villa away. Their tie with Shakhtar Donetsk is set for Stamford Bridge in London.

Each club plays eight games, four of them at home, under the expanded format. Galatasaray also drew Sporting, Feyenoord, Lille, Stuttgart and AEK, while Fenerbahçe got Villarreal, Slavia Prague and LASK.
