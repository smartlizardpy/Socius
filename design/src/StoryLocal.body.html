KADIKÖY · PADEL

Three new courts open on the Fenerbahçe side

[[icon:newspaper:15]] Socius · 28 August

Three covered padel courts opened this week off Bağdat Caddesi, taking the number in Kadıköy to eleven.

Off-peak hours run at ₺520 for the court — ₺130 a player for doubles, around 15% under the Caddebostan rate.

The new courts are filling through September, with two a night held back for open games.
