# Socius Halı Saha UGC Ad — Creative Concept

> This document is a creative brief only. It is **not** an implementation plan and contains no Remotion code.

## The core idea

A Turkish vertical UGC ad that begins like a real person spontaneously calling out a familiar halı saha problem, shifts into a fast premium presentation of the **real Socius product**, and returns to the same person or a clean brand finish.

The feeling should be:

- relatable enough to stop the scroll;
- attractive and aspirational without looking like a polished TV commercial;
- unmistakably about halı saha football;
- fast, tactile, and satisfying during the product section;
- one continuous story, carried by the same ElevenLabs voice.

The simple promise:

**Stop chasing missing players in WhatsApp groups. Find a suitable match and get on the pitch.**

## Format and production constraints

- Platform: TikTok / Reels / Shorts
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Target length: roughly 25–28 seconds
- AI-video allowance: two 5-second Higgsfield clips
- Voice: one consistent ElevenLabs Turkish voice across the entire ad
- Middle section: animated presentation using real Socius screens and assets
- Language: natural conversational Turkish, not corporate Turkish

## Structure

### 1. UGC hook — approximately 0:00–0:05

A beautiful blonde woman in her twenties stands beside a halı saha in İzmir. She has a believable Aegean / Greek-Turkish look, sporty casual styling, and speaks directly to the phone camera. The environment feels real: evening floodlights, green pitch, players softly moving in the background, a little handheld phone movement.

Hook:

> **“Halı saha yapmak istiyorsun… ama yine iki kişi eksik, değil mi?”**

Why it works: it names the exact frustration immediately. There is no app explanation yet.

On-screen captions should emphasize **“iki kişi eksik”**.

### 2. Product presentation — approximately 0:05–0:22

Her ElevenLabs voice continues over the visuals. This is **not a plain screen recording** and not a fake app mockup. It is a premium product-film treatment made from the latest real Socius screens.

Narrative flow:

1. **Find football nearby**  
   The Socius discovery screen enters. The football / halı saha filter is selected. Nearby matches become the visual focus.

2. **Choose what fits you**  
   The camera travels into a real match card. Time, location, skill level, price, and remaining player slots are revealed in sequence.

3. **Know who actually shows up**  
   Participant profiles fan out. Attendance or reliability information is highlighted. This is the trust-building differentiator: you can see who is joining and whether they normally attend.

4. **Join in one move**  
   A decisive tap on the real join CTA. The final missing-player slot fills, the player count completes, and the state changes to a satisfying confirmation.

Suggested voiceover:

> “Socius’ta yakınındaki halı saha maçlarını buluyorsun. Saatine ve seviyene uyan maçı seçiyorsun. Katılmadan önce kimlerin geleceğini, hatta oyuncuların maçlara gerçekten gelip gelmediğini görebiliyorsun. Sana uyan maça tek dokunuşla katılıyorsun.”

This wording is a draft. Before production, time it against the final ElevenLabs recording and shorten it rather than accelerating the voice unnaturally.

### 3. Closing payoff — approximately 0:22–0:27

Preferred close:

> **“Grubun tamamlanmasını bekleme. Maçını bul. Sahaya çık.”**

Finish with the real Socius mark and one direct CTA:

> **“Socius’ta bul.”**

The second 5-second Higgsfield credit can be used in one of two ways:

- preferred: return to the same woman at the side of the pitch for a human closing line, then cut to the Socius mark;
- safer continuity option: use a short non-speaking reaction—she looks toward the pitch, smiles, and walks in—while the ElevenLabs voice delivers the close.

The non-speaking ending avoids a second lip-sync risk and feels more cinematic.

## Product-animation direction

The middle should feel like a moving editorial presentation, not someone slowly operating a phone.

Use:

- the **actual latest Socius app screens**;
- the established design system and **TasteMaster** decisions;
- real typography, colors, spacing, cards, icons, photos, and copy;
- layered 2.5D depth made from exported screen components;
- controlled camera pushes, pans, crops, and match cuts;
- small tactile interactions: filter selection, card lift, avatar fan-out, CTA press, slot filled;
- occasional oversized Turkish words behind the interface, used sparingly: “YAKININDA”, “SANA UYGUN”, “KİM GELİYOR?”;
- motion synchronized to sentence beats and important consonants, not constant random movement.

Do not use:

- invented or approximate Socius UI;
- a generic phone floating in the center for the whole ad;
- long static screenshots;
- excessive glassmorphism, neon, particles, or template-like transitions;
- tiny app text that cannot be read on a phone;
- every feature in the product;
- English interface copy in a Turkish ad;
- fast motion merely to make the video feel energetic.

The camera should guide one visual idea at a time. Each shot needs a clear focal point.

## Visual transition idea

The opening woman or camera moves close enough that a dark jacket, hand, or football briefly fills the frame. That shape becomes the transition into a similarly colored element on the Socius screen. At the end, a circular UI element or player avatar can match-cut back to the football or the pitch floodlight.

This connects UGC and product presentation without a generic flash or swipe transition.

## Higgsfield prompt — opening shot

Use the approved character reference if available so her identity stays consistent.

> Vertical 9:16 authentic Turkish UGC video, five continuous seconds. A strikingly beautiful blonde woman in her mid twenties with an Aegean Turkish / Greek-descended İzmir appearance stands naturally beside an outdoor halı saha football pitch at blue hour. Long blonde hair, warm olive-fair skin, expressive face, tasteful sporty casual outfit, subtle natural makeup. She looks directly into a smartphone camera and talks conversationally as if sharing a useful secret with friends. Real green artificial turf, wire fencing and bright floodlights behind her, a few football players softly out of focus in the background. Slight handheld phone movement, realistic exposure shifts, natural blinking and head movement, confident playful energy, authentic social-media footage, not a commercial, not a fashion shoot. Medium close-up, eye-level camera, clean face visibility, no cuts, no camera orbit, no dramatic gestures, no subtitles, no logos, no text.

Important performance note: keep the spoken delivery calm and compact. Large mouth movements usually make imperfect Turkish lip sync more noticeable.

## Higgsfield prompt — optional closing shot

> Vertical 9:16 authentic Turkish UGC continuation using exactly the same woman, face, hair, outfit, location, time of day and smartphone-camera look as the opening clip. She hears the game starting, gives the camera a small knowing smile, turns toward the illuminated halı saha and walks naturally toward the pitch while holding a football at her side. Slight handheld movement, real background players, natural body motion, warm confident energy, one continuous five-second shot, no speaking, no cuts, no logos, no text.

## ElevenLabs voice design prompt

> A young adult Turkish woman from İzmir with a warm, clear, naturally attractive voice. Conversational social-media delivery, confident and lightly playful, like recommending something genuinely useful to a friend. Native Turkish pronunciation, modern Istanbul Turkish with a subtle relaxed Aegean warmth. Medium pitch, smooth tone, crisp consonants, natural breaths, and varied sentence rhythm. Energetic enough to hold attention but never salesy, theatrical, childish, or overly cheerful. She should sound human, close to the microphone, and comfortable speaking over a fast visual product presentation.

Delivery notes:

- record the complete ad as one voice file so tone and pacing stay consistent;
- use short sentences and natural pauses;
- emphasize “iki kişi eksik”, “kimlerin geleceğini”, and “tek dokunuşla”;
- do not solve timing by making the voice unnaturally fast;
- keep music under the voice and use sound effects only to support visual actions.

## Lip-sync fallback

If the selected video model produces weak Turkish lip movement:

1. generate the opening with minimal, relaxed mouth movement;
2. keep the line very short;
3. use a dedicated Turkish-compatible lip-sync tool only if the result is convincing;
4. otherwise hide the final words behind a natural camera move or cut to the pitch while the voice continues;
5. make the closing Higgsfield shot non-speaking.

The ad does not need perfect visible speech for all five seconds. It needs a convincing first second and a seamless voice-led transition.

## Sound direction

- one continuous ElevenLabs narration across UGC, product, and close;
- subtle halı saha ambience under the opening and ending;
- restrained rhythmic music under the product section;
- tactile sounds for filter select, card focus, reliability reveal, CTA tap, and player-slot completion;
- no loud whoosh on every transition.

## Source-of-truth rule for the next production session

Before designing any animation, the agent must inspect:

1. the latest running Socius app;
2. the existing design documentation;
3. the TasteMaster output / taste decisions;
4. the exact football discovery, match-detail, participant, and join states.

It should export or reproduce those screens faithfully, then plan motion around them. If a required state does not exist, stop and flag it instead of inventing a replacement UI.

## Definition of success

The finished ad should make a Turkish halı saha player think, within a few seconds:

> “Evet, bu sürekli başıma geliyor—and this app could actually get me into a game tonight.”
